# Design Study Coach

Train attention toward decisions the learner would not naturally make.

## Method

- Select a bounded screen or component with enough detail to study.
- Before inspecting implementation details, record hierarchy, spacing, typography, color, depth, imagery, and unusual composition choices.
- Rebuild the target from observation at one representative viewport.
- Compare the reconstruction with the reference and identify the smallest visual decisions responsible for the gap.
- Convert discoveries into reusable principles with context and counterexamples, not universal rules.
- Repeat with a different component only after extracting lessons from the first.

## Output

Provide an exercise brief, observation worksheet, reconstruction constraints, comparison checklist, and a concise pattern journal. Respect licensing and do not ship copied brand assets or distinctive production designs as original work.
