# Spacing and Layout

Use space to reveal relationships and give each region only the room it needs.

## Review

- Begin from generous whitespace, then remove it deliberately where density serves the task.
- Use a constrained spacing scale instead of arbitrary one-pixel adjustments.
- Keep space within a group smaller than space between groups.
- Avoid stretching forms, prose, cards, or controls merely to fill the viewport.
- Use columns to balance a wide canvas without making narrow content harder to use.
- Replace unnecessary separating borders with spacing, background differences, or a restrained shadow.
- Check horizontal and vertical grouping, not only vertical rhythm.

## Output

Identify ambiguous grouping and the smallest spacing or width changes. Include size-specific checks where relevant. Preserve information density when the product depends on seeing many items at once.
